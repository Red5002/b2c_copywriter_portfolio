Case Study: ClipForge

ClipForge helps creators turn long YouTube videos into viral TikTok clips in under 10 seconds. Here is how I structured the copy to move users from passive observers to active trial users.

01. Problem

"Repurposing content takes longer than filming it."

Creators are exhausted. I focused the copy on the friction of editing—hours spent scrubbing through timelines just to find 30 good seconds.

02. Desire

"One click. Ten seconds. Ten clips."

I translated 'AI identification algorithms' into a simple, tangible result: getting your time back while your social presence grows on autopilot.

03. Reassurance

"Works with any link. No credit card required."

To lower the barrier, the copy emphasizes ease of entry. No complex setup, just paste a link and see the magic.

04. Action

"Clip your first video for free."

A low-friction CTA that promises an immediate win rather than a generic 'Sign Up' button.

Case Study: SubZero

SubZero is a consumer SaaS that finds and cancels hidden subscriptions. This spec piece showcases direct, pain-led positioning, problem agitation, onboarding email copy, and cancellation UX microcopy.

01. Positioning

"Stop paying for apps you forgot existed."

Avoided generic claims like "financial freedom" in favor of an immediately recognizable, highly specific pain point that requires no mental friction to grasp.

02. Agitation

Exposing dark patterns and silent charges

Agitated three specific consumer scenarios: forgotten 7-day trials, stealth $9-to-$14 price hikes, and multi-step cancellation traps engineered to make users give up.

03. Lifecycle Email

"We found $47/month in hidden charges..."

Drafted an automated "First Scan Complete" email that quantifies annual financial leaks ($500+/year) to drive 1-click re-engagement back to the app.

04. UX Microcopy

Frictionless Cancellation & Reassurance

Designed modal microcopy setting explicit billing cycle expectations alongside instant positive feedback highlighting exact total dollars saved.
